# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Robotics researchers and operators running controlled Unitree G1 experiments across a robot, a GB10 inference workstation, and a laptop. They need to verify system health, observe perception and control decisions, and preserve structured evidence from each experiment.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy browser-based research console for RealSense-guided plushie detection and pregrasp experiments. Success means an operator can understand what every stage is doing, recognize why a target was accepted or rejected, access the system from the local network or an SSH tunnel, and export session telemetry without mixing it with model-training outputs.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, evidence-first. The interface should feel like dependable laboratory instrumentation: dense enough for research, legible at a glance, and explicit about uncertainty and safety state.

## Anti-references

Avoid entertainment-style robot dashboards, decorative sci-fi HUDs, unexplained red/green indicators, oversized vanity metrics, and camera viewers that hide the underlying timing, calibration, detection, and control data.

## Design Principles

- Show the full observation-to-decision chain, not just the camera image.
- Make stale, missing, rejected, and unsafe states explicit and diagnosable.
- Keep operator actions separate from read-only research observation.
- Preserve structured experiment evidence with clear session identity and export paths.
- Prefer familiar monitoring and table patterns over novel controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-accessible links and controls, visible focus states, semantic status text in addition to color, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
